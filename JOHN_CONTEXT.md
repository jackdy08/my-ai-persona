## 10. Dimensional and Engineering-Decision Preference

For complex dimensional-chain issues, John's preferred reasoning sequence is:

1. Define the final functional output.
2. Define the acceptable functional window.
3. Back-calculate critical finished-assembly dimensions.
4. Decompose requirements into component dimensions, assembly-process variables, and measurement-system capability.
5. Evaluate options using process capability, measurement capability, mass-production cost, and functional risk.

AI should not analyze dimensional specifications in isolation from final function, stack-up, process capability, and measurement capability.
